# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ProvenHire serves candidates completing timed, proctored assessments; workspace organizers and hiring teams reviewing persisted evidence; and platform administrators operating the assessment pipeline. Candidates need to understand what is required, complete each round without losing work, and see an honest, useful report. Reviewers need comparable evidence, clear provenance, and explicit limits before making a human hiring decision.

## Product Purpose

ProvenHire is a skill-certified hiring network. Its workspace product coordinates aptitude, coding, SQL or data, and structured interview rounds, retains the underlying evidence, and turns that evidence into explainable candidate reports. Success means every configured round is reachable and recoverable, every displayed claim can be traced to retained evidence, sparse evidence is labeled as such, and generated interpretations remain advisory rather than making the hiring decision.

## Brand Personality

Evidence-led, calm, accountable. The product should feel rigorous without becoming intimidating, and candid about uncertainty without becoming vague. Copy should be direct, operational, and respectful of both candidates and reviewers.

## Anti-references

- Black-box scoring that presents a confident hiring verdict without showing evidence or coverage.
- Assessment products that conflate passed judge cases with completed tasks, or aggregate scores with item-level performance.
- Inconsistent round experiences where aptitude, coding, SQL, and interview reports use unrelated states, labels, or navigation.
- Decorative dashboard styling that competes with the candidate's next action or the reviewer's evidence trail.
- Long synchronous AI requests that strand the user on a loading state or require them to keep a page open.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before interpretation: preserve the ledger, source, judge cases, transcript-derived observations, and provenance before generating summaries.
2. Confidence follows coverage: a score from a short fixture or lightly tested interview must never look equivalent to a full assessment.
3. One round lifecycle: every module uses the same not-started, in-progress, processing, complete, failed, and retry language.
4. Recoverable by default: long-running reports continue in the background, survive navigation, and remain discoverable in the workspace.
5. Humans own decisions: reports organize facts, contradictions, and coaching guidance; a named reviewer applies the role rubric and records the decision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across candidate and administrative workspace surfaces. Do not rely on color alone for status, maintain keyboard-visible focus, preserve readable contrast and touch targets, support reduced motion, and use plain-language explanations for proctoring, evidence limitations, failures, and retries. Assessment timing and integrity controls must not erase saved work or prevent an authorized accommodation path.
